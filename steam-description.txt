[h1]Disable Durability[/h1]

Tools, weapons, and armor in Core Keeper slowly wear down as you use them. This mod stops that — while it is active, items no longer lose durability.

Each item's durability bar simply stays where it is: an item at 100/100 stays full, an item at 30/100 stays at 30/100. Nothing you carry will break from use again.

[b]Not a repair mod.[/b] Disable Durability [i]freezes[/i] durability — it does not refill it. Items keep whatever durability they currently have; they will not regenerate back to maximum. Get an item to full durability once and it stays full for good.

[b]Multiplayer:[/b] install the mod on both the client and the server.

[h2]Settings[/h2]

Toggle the mod on or off in-game under Options → Mod settings — no need to uninstall it to let durability work normally for a while.
