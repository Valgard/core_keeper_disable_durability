# Disable Durability

Tools, weapons and armour stop wearing down. A bar at 100/100 stays there, a
bar at 30/100 stays at 30/100 — nothing you carry breaks from use again.

Worth knowing before you install: this **freezes** durability, it does not
repair. An item keeps whatever it has now and will not climb back up. Get
something to full once and it stays full for good.

## Settings

**Options → Mod settings** has an on/off switch, so durability can work
normally for a while without uninstalling anything.

## Requirements

**Mod Settings Menu** and **CoreLib**, both offered when you subscribe.

## Multiplayer

Install on both the client and the server.
